# Unit 5: Project Management and Quality

## 10 Golden Points
1. Size metrics: productivity is KLOC per person month, plus errors per KLOC, defects per KLOC and cost per LOC
2. Defect removal efficiency DRE equals E divided by E plus D, where E is errors before release and D is defects after release
3. Failure percentage is failures divided by runs into 100, reliability is 100 minus failure percentage, MTBF is MTTF plus MTTR
4. Function point: UFP is the sum of count into weight for EI, EO, EQ, ILF, EIF. VAF is 0.65 plus 0.01 into sum of Fi. FP is UFP into VAF
5. Basic COCOMO: Effort equals a into KLOC power b, Time equals c into Effort power d. Modes are organic, semi detached, embedded
6. Intermediate COCOMO: Effort equals a into KLOC power b into EAF, and EAF is the product of all cost drivers
7. CPM: forward pass gives ES and EF, backward pass gives LS and LF, slack is LS minus ES, critical path has zero slack
8. Risk exposure RE equals probability into cost. Known risks come from the plan, predictable risks from past projects, unpredictable cannot be seen in advance
9. McCall has 11 factors in 3 groups: Product Operation 5, Product Revision 3, Product Transition 3
10. CMM has 5 levels: Initial, Repeatable, Defined, Managed, Optimizing. Quality dilemma means aim for good enough, not perfect
