# Unit 3: Design, Architecture, Process Models

## 10 Golden Points
1. Good design means high cohesion inside a module and low coupling between modules
2. Coupling from best to worst: Data, Stamp, Control, External, Common, Content
3. Cohesion from worst to best: Coincidental, Logical, Temporal, Procedural, Communicational, Sequential, Functional
4. Structure chart is derived from DFD using transform analysis or transaction analysis
5. Architecture styles: layered, client server, MVC, pipe and filter, microservices, event driven, repository
6. Architecture decision template: design issue, resolution, category, constraints, alternatives, argument, implications, related decisions, work products, notes
7. Waterfall is sequential with no going back. Its problems are fixed requirements, late working software and costly changes
8. V model pairs each development phase with a test phase: requirements with acceptance, design with system, architecture with integration, module with unit
9. Spiral model has 4 quadrants every loop: planning, risk analysis, engineering, evaluation. It handles risk best
10. Pick the model by need: clear requirements Waterfall, unclear Prototype, early release Incremental, high risk Spiral, speed RAD

## Worked calculations

### Scene 3: High cohesion + low coupling = easy to change, test, reuse
    ## Coupling vs Cohesion (PYQ 3a)
    Cohesion: inside one module
    Coupling: between modules
    Goal: high cohesion, low coupling
    High cohesion: one job, easy to understand
    Low coupling: change in one does not break others
    Impact: maintainability, reusability, testability
    ~ Content coupling and coincidental cohesion are the worst

### Scene 7: Waterfall: ek baar bird chhoda, wapas nahi aata!
    ## Problems of Waterfall (PYQ mid 2a)
    1 Requirements must be fixed in the beginning
    2 Working software comes very late
    3 Change is costly, no going back
    4 Risk and bugs found late
    5 Customer sees nothing till the end
    ## Good for
    Small projects with clear, stable requirements
